Our story

Built in Humboldt. Grown in Humboldt.

A Humboldt contractor family that built cannabis businesses with their own hands, lost some, and came back with a place designed around how people feel when they walk in.

Humboldt AF Cannabis is a farm to table Cannabis company.

Our products are currently available for retail purchase at our dispensary storefronts, Humboldt's Premium Arcata 460 I St, Arcata Ca 95521, and Humboldt's Premium at 1131 Broadway, Eureka CA, 95501.

We also offer delivery service from Trinidad to Rio Dell.

The road here

From a collective to the courtyard.

Drag or scroll the timeline. Every line comes from the record: magazine features, the state license and the city's own votes.

  1. ~2010s

    Professional contractor James Alves and his wife Amy turned to cannabis after construction dipped following the real estate crash: a collective, then a farm (Humboldt AF Cannabis), then two delivery services, Humboldt's Magic Bus and Chico Magic Bus.

  2. Then

    Like most enterprises in this unpredictable industry, they had to close the doors and re-evaluate.

  3. 2015

    The company dates its community service from this year.

  4. 2018

    Microbusiness established at 1131 Broadway, Eureka: manufacturing, distribution and local delivery first, then the dispensary.

  5. 2020

    Eureka storefront opens.

  6. 2022

    City approves an outdoor consumption patio for the Arcata site (non-smoking use).

  7. June 2023

    Arcata retail license issued (C10-0001327-LIC, adult-use and medicinal).

  8. Summer 2023

    Second store opens at 460 I St, Arcata, in the former V&N Burger Bar building.

  9. Aug 24, 2024

    First-anniversary party: DJ, vendors, games, spin-the-wheel, in-store specials.

  10. April 2026

    Arcata City Council legalizes on-site smoking.

  11. Nov 10, 2026

    Arcata Planning Commission unanimously approves an outdoor smoking lounge on the patio.

Sources: California Leaf (Feb 2025), the Arcata Chamber listing, the store's Facebook page, the California DCC license record and the Lost Coast Outpost (Nov 9 and 11, 2026).

The builders

A contractor built this store.

James and Amy took inspiration from one of their favorite shows, "Bar Rescue", to lay the shop out from the customer's point of view.

The result is what you see now: slat boards with vibrant colors and floating lights from the ceiling, a clean and classy place to get your goodies, and a courtyard out back where townspeople are encouraged to hang with friends. For those who grew up here, it used to be the V&N Burger Bar.
