Glossary

Say it like a budtender.

30 terms you will hear at the counter, in a sentence or two each.

5ABCDEHILMPRST

5

510 thread
The standard screw thread used by most vape cartridges and batteries, so a 510 cartridge fits most 510 batteries.

A

AIO (all-in-one)
A vape with the battery and the oil in one device, so there's nothing to match up.

B

Badder
A concentrate whipped to a soft, creamy texture, a bit like cake frosting. Sometimes spelled batter.

C

Caryophyllene
A terpene with a peppery, spicy aroma, also found in black pepper and cloves.
CBD
Cannabidiol, a cannabinoid that doesn't produce a high the way THC does. Many products pair it with THC, so check the ratio on the label.
CBN
Cannabinol, a cannabinoid that forms as THC ages. It often shows up in evening-themed products, though research on it is still limited.
Cultivar (strain)
A specific variety of cannabis, like Blue Dream or GMO. Cultivar is the grower's word; strain is the everyday one.

D

Decarboxylation
The heating process that turns THCA into THC. It happens when you light or vape flower, and edible makers do it on purpose before infusing.
Diamonds
Crystals of nearly pure THCA, often sold sitting in sauce. They're among the strongest products on the menu.
Dry sift
A traditional, solventless hash made by sifting dried flower over fine screens to collect the resin glands.

E

Eighth, quarter and zip
Common flower weights: an eighth is 1/8 ounce (about 3.5 grams), a quarter is 1/4 ounce (about 7 grams), and zip is slang for a full ounce (about 28 grams). Zip is also a bag name on our menu and in our deals, so a Quarter Zip or a 1/4 oz Zip is 7 grams, not a full ounce.
Entourage effect
The theory that cannabinoids and terpenes work together to shape the experience, rather than THC alone. It's a popular idea, and the research is still developing.

H

Hash
Resin glands (trichomes) collected from the plant and kept loose or pressed together. It's one of the oldest forms of cannabis concentrate.
Hybrid
A cross of indica and sativa genetics, which covers most cannabis on shelves today. On our menu it lines up with Balance.

I

Indica
A traditional label linked with a heavier, more relaxed feel. On our menu it lines up with Relax, as a loose guide rather than a promise.

L

Limonene
A terpene with a bright citrus aroma, also found in lemon and orange peel.
Linalool
A terpene with a soft floral aroma, best known from lavender.
Live resin
A concentrate made from plants frozen fresh at harvest instead of dried and cured, to hold on to more of the fresh aroma.

M

Microdose
Taking a very small amount of THC, often 2.5 mg or less, to keep effects light.
Mixed light
Cannabis grown in a greenhouse or hoop house using the sun plus some help, like blackout covers or extra lights.
Myrcene
A common terpene with an earthy, musky aroma, also found in hops and mango.

P

Pinene
A terpene that smells like fresh pine needles, also found in rosemary.
Pre-roll
Flower that comes already rolled and ready to light. An infused pre-roll adds concentrate inside or on the outside, which makes it much stronger.

R

Rosin
A solventless concentrate pressed from flower or hash with heat and pressure. Live rosin starts with fresh-frozen plants.

S

Sativa
A traditional label linked with a lighter, more upbeat feel. On our menu it lines up with Uplift, as a loose guide rather than a promise.
Sauce
A runny, aromatic concentrate, often with THCA crystals mixed in.
Sun-grown
Cannabis grown outdoors under the sun, with no artificial light. It usually means one harvest a year, in the fall.

T

Terpenes
Aromatic compounds that give cannabis its smell and flavor, from the same family found in citrus, pine and lavender. Many people believe they help shape the experience, though the research is still early.
THC
Tetrahydrocannabinol, the main compound in cannabis that causes a high. Labels list it as a percentage for flower and concentrates, and in milligrams for edibles and drinks.
THCA
The raw, non-intoxicating form of THC found in the plant. Heat from a flame, a vape or an oven turns it into THC.
