Edibles and Drinks: Start Low, Go Slow

Edibles and drinks are easy to enjoy and easy to overdo, so here's how to read the label and pace yourself.

The one rule

If you remember one thing, make it this: start low, go slow. Edibles and drinks are an easy, smoke-free way to enjoy cannabis, but they work differently than smoking. The most common mistake is taking more before the first dose has kicked in.

Why edibles take their time

When you smoke or vape, you usually feel it within minutes. When you eat cannabis, it goes through your digestive system first, and that takes a while. Edibles can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 2 hours to kick in, and the effects can last several hours.

Lots of things change the timing: your body, what you've eaten, the product itself and how often you use cannabis. Two people can eat the same gummy and have very different evenings.

Drinks often feel sooner

Many people find that cannabis drinks kick in sooner than a classic edible. Sooner isn't the same as instant, though, and a drink is still measured in milligrams like any edible. Sip slowly, set the can down for a while and see how you feel before you open another. It's also smart to skip the alcohol when you're enjoying a cannabis drink.

Reading the milligram label

Every edible and drink lists its THC in milligrams (mg). Look for two numbers:

  1. Total mg in the package. This is usually the big number on the front.
  2. Mg per piece or per serving. This is the amount you actually take.

Do the quick math. A 20-piece pack labeled 100 mg works out to 5 mg a piece. A 10-piece pack labeled 100 mg is 10 mg a piece. Drinks vary a lot: on our menu, some hold 10 mg in the whole can, while others hold 100 mg, which is several servings. Some products also list CBD, CBN or other cannabinoids with a ratio like 1:1.

A few products carry much more per piece. Some concentrate tablets list 25 mg, 50 mg or even 100 mg each. Those aren't a first-timer's product.

A simple first-time plan

  • Start small. Many first-timers start around 2.5 to 5 mg of THC. Half a piece is a perfectly good start.
  • Wait at least 2 hours before taking more. Set a timer if it helps.
  • Pick a relaxed setting with nothing important on the schedule, and don't drive.
  • Keep notes. Write down what you took, how much and when, so next time is easier.

If you feel too high, find a calm, comfortable spot, sip some water and give it time. If you're ever worried about yourself or someone else, call 911.

Storing edibles and drinks safely

Edibles and drinks can look just like regular candy and soda, so safe storage matters.

  • Keep them in their original child-resistant packaging.
  • Store them up high, locked if you can, away from kids and pets.
  • Give cannabis drinks their own spot in the fridge, away from regular sodas.
  • Keep the label so you always know what's inside and how strong it is.

If a child gets into cannabis, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222. If a pet does, call your vet. In an emergency, call 911.

Enjoying them in our courtyard

Our courtyard welcomes edibles and THC drinks today. If you'd like to enjoy something out back, give it time to kick in, pace yourself and plan your ride home before you start: a sober driver, a walk or a ride. Read up on courtyard manners before you head out back, and browse edibles and drinks anytime.

The short version

  • Many first-timers start around 2.5 to 5 mg of THC and wait at least 2 hours before taking more.
  • Drinks often feel sooner than edibles, but read the mg label either way.
  • Store edibles and drinks in child-resistant packaging, away from kids and pets.
