# Media and provenance

Real client media first. AI imagery never stands in for the store, staff or customers; three owner photos were relit
to dusk or night with AI (layout kept). AI files carry `data-ai="generated"` or `data-ai="edited"`, an alt text that
describes the pixels, and the footer note. Product photos on category pages are the live store's own product images.

## Models

- Higgsfield: Marketing Studio Flare (scene stills, category renders, floating sprigs, the cinematic sunset relight),
  Kling 3.0 Pro image-to-video (the courtyard dusk loop and the fire-table loop, first frame = last frame).
- fal: Nano Banana Pro edit (faithful dusk/night relights of the owner's courtyard photo, golden-hour waterfall),
  BiRefNet v2 (transparent cut-outs).
