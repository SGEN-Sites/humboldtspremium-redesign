# Humboldt's Premium Arcata: redesign pitch notes

Design concept preview, built 2026-11-20. Every page carries `noindex`. Shop and cart buttons open the live store.

## What we kept, word for word

- Every live URL: `/`, `/arcata-home` (now forwards to `/`), `/arcata-details`, `/about-us`, `/shop` and all 15 category
  pages, including the live spelling `/a-tintctures`.
- The live words: the welcome lines, hours, ordering line, payments, ID rule, tax line, exchange and store policies,
  "We have the best selection of weed in Arcata and Humboldt.", "Voted the best dispensary.", the About text, the
  "Prem. Team" (Dustin, Justin, Misha, James), "JOIN US", the two phone numbers and both email addresses, the age-check
  buttons ("YES" I am 21 or over / "NO" I am under 21) and the footer licence line.
- The whole menu: 316 products in 15 categories, with names, prices, sale prices and ribbons as listed on the live
  store on 2026-11-20. Every category page lists its own products; every **Add to cart** opens that product on the live
  store, where the bag and checkout live. The bag icon opens the live cart.
- The promotions that were only inside images (Daily Deals, November promotions, Spin the Wheel), now typed out as
  real text so search engines and screen readers can read them.

## What is new

- A real home page behind the age check (the live front door is only the age gate).
- The courtyard and the newly approved outdoor lounge as the hero: `/courtyard-lounge/` with the approval story.
- Our Story with a sourced timeline, values drawn from what the family does, and the team.
- Daily deals board (today lights up automatically), sale items, monthly promos and the wheel: `/deals/`.
- Free home delivery hub plus six town pages (McKinleyville, Eureka, Blue Lake, Fortuna, Ferndale, Carlotta).
- Learn hub with 8 guides and a 30-term glossary, a 22-question FAQ, What's on, Locations, Contact,
  a "before or after the Marsh" page, an HTML site map and a 404.
- Shop by feeling (Relax, Balance, Uplift) mapped to the store's own indica, hybrid and sativa shelves.
- A sticky pickup bar (past the hero, hidden at the footer, closable for the visit).

## Defects found on the live site

- The footer gives the Arcata store's address as "Eureka CA 95501".
- The licence appears two ways: C10-0001327-LIC (footer) and C10-001327-LIC (age-check block).
- "Thu rsday" on Arcata Details; "Come in a enjoy" in the meta description, which is the same on every page.
- No H1 on any page; the home page title is "Arcata Age Check" and its description is the age-check line.
- Deals and promotions exist only as images: invisible to search and screen readers.
- Category labels carry an "A " prefix in the menu; the tinctures URL is misspelled (kept, to preserve the URL).
