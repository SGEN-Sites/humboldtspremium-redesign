Our story

Built by a Humboldt contractor family.

James Alves was a professional contractor. When construction dipped after the real estate crash, he and his wife Amy turned to cannabis: a collective, a farm, two delivery services.

Like most enterprises in this unpredictable industry, they had to close the doors and re-evaluate. They came back with retail: Eureka in 2020, then this shop on I Street, laid out from the customer's point of view and built by James himself.

Good to know

Questions from the counter.

The things people ask us most. More in the full FAQ.

All questions
What are your hours?

Our posted hours are 9 am to 9 pm Monday through Saturday, and 10 am to 7 pm on Sunday. Hours can change, so check our Visit page or call 707-630-4098 before a special trip.

What forms of payment do you take?

Cash and debit. Debit has a $3.00 fee. Sorry, no credit.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Place an order online from our Shop page, or call it in at 707-630-4098, then pick it up in store. Bring your ID.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We offer delivery service from Trinidad to Rio Dell, including towns like Arcata, Eureka, McKinleyville, Blue Lake, Fortuna, Ferndale and Carlotta. To check your address, call 707-630-4098 or email delivery@humboldtafc.com. More on our Delivery page.

Is delivery free? Is there a minimum?

Our site lists free home delivery. Minimums and delivery hours aren't posted, so call 707-630-4098 or email delivery@humboldtafc.com and we'll fill you in.

Can you deliver to a parking lot or park?

No. Our policy: no sales to parking lots or alleyways or other public areas. Have your ID ready when your order arrives.
